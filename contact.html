Google Play Data Safety & User Privacy Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's Account & Data Deletion policy and applicable privacy standards, users of UPSC Audiobook (com.setunotes.upscaudiobook) can easily request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal study data.

Option 1

Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

If you still have the UPSC Audiobook app installed on your Android device:

  1. 1
    Open the UPSC Audiobook App on your device.
  2. 2
    Tap the Profile or Settings icon in the navigation bar.
  3. 3
    Navigate to Account Security & Privacy.
  4. 4
    Select Delete Account & Study Data and confirm.
ℹ️ This will immediately unlink your account and trigger the automated deletion pipeline.

📋 Transparency & Data Safety

What Data Is Deleted vs Retained?

We value your privacy and practice data minimization. Here is a clear breakdown of what happens when you request account deletion for the UPSC Audiobook app:

🗑️

Data Permanently Deleted

Removed within 30 days
  • User Profile & Authentication: Google ID, Name, Registered Email address, Avatar/Profile Photo.
  • Audiobook Listening Progress: Chapter timestamps, playback completion status, speed preferences, and downloaded chapter logs.
  • AI Mains Answer Evaluations: Uploaded answer sheets, handwritten images, transcribed answer text, AI feedback, score ratings, and suggestions.
  • Prelims Mock Test Data: Attempt history, quiz scores, accuracy analytics, bookmarks, and revision flags.
  • Personal Notes & Highlights: All custom audio notes, chapter bookmarks, and highlighted syllabus tags.
  • Device Identifiers & Push Tokens: FCM notification tokens, device models, OS versions, and session tokens.
⚖️

Data Retained (If Applicable)

Strict legal & operational limits
  • Financial & Transaction Records (If any): If you made in-app purchases or donations, invoice records are retained for the statutory period required by Indian Tax and Accounting Laws (e.g., GST / Income Tax Act) and then purged.
  • Anonymized / Aggregated Telemetry: Aggregated app crash rates, performance metrics, and general analytics that are completely anonymized and cannot be linked back to any individual user.
  • Deletion Audit Log: A timestamped hash of the deletion request record (with PII redacted) to verify compliance with Google Play Store policies and privacy laws.
⏱️

Deletion Timeline & Verification Protocol

1. Verification: We may send a verification email to your registered address to ensure the request is authorized by the actual account holder.
2. Cooling-off Period: If requested via web, a 7-day grace period applies in case you submitted by mistake.
3. Complete Purge: All personal data across production databases and replica backups is permanently deleted within 30 days maximum, complying with Google Play Data Safety requirements.
